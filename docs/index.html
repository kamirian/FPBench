Foundation Potential Benchmark

FPBench

FPBench evaluates foundation potentials (FPs) using application-oriented metrics across three computational materials workflows: force prediction, phase stability and elemental ordering, and ion migration by NEB calculations.

Components

Available

Force Prediction

Force-error metrics for FPs on MatPES-PBE, MatPES-r2SCAN, and OMat24 rattled-1000, covering highly accurate force predictions, joint magnitude-angle accuracy, large-force-error atoms, and far-from-equilibrium force errors.
